# Platform Overview

## 🎮 What is This Platform?

A **gaming services marketplace** where gamers can buy and sell game boosting services across multiple games.

Think of it like **G2G.com** or **Fiverr for gaming** - but specifically designed for game boosting services like:
- Mythic+ dungeon runs
- Character leveling
- Raid completions
- Achievement unlocks
- Custom game services

---

## 🎯 The Problem We Solve

### For Buyers (Gamers)
**Problem:** "I don't have time to grind in my favorite game, but I want the rewards."

**Solution:** Buy services from professional boosters who will complete the content for you.

### For Service Providers (Boosters & Advertisers)
**Problem:** "I'm good at games and want to earn money, but I need a platform to find customers."

**Solution:** Create service listings, get matched with buyers, and earn money doing what you love.

### For Platform Owners
**Problem:** "I want to run a gaming services marketplace but need a secure, scalable platform."

**Solution:** Complete platform with user management, financial controls, and team collaboration features.

## 💰 How Money Flows

```
Buyer purchases service (500 Gold)
         ↓
Buyer's wallet: -500 Gold
         ↓
Platform holds payment
         ↓
Booster completes service
         ↓
Service provider's wallet: +500 Gold
         ↓
Provider can withdraw to real money
```

### Admin Approval Required For:
- ✅ Withdrawals (taking money out)
- ✅ Role requests (becoming booster/advertiser)

### Instant (No Approval):
- ✅ Deposits (adding money) - Instant via payment gateway
- ✅ Currency conversion
- ✅ Service purchases
- ✅ Team collaboration
